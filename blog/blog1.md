What is Data Science to Me? Why is it Important to learn Data Science in Social and Interdisciplinary Contexts? 

  To me, Data Science is using computer science, statistics, mathematics, and other methods to turn messy data into useful information that can be used to make smart decisions. Learning data science in social and interdisciplinary contexts matters because data is much more than just numbers. Data represents people, cultures, systems, institutions, and much more. Additionally, social context can help reveal biases in data and highlight when supposedly objective algorithms are actually reinforcing existing inequalities. Since data science is connected to so many fields, like public health and economics, it is important to analyze the data to identify patterns and understand why they exist. Therefore, it is important to model social topics and people because it can help identify patterns in human behavior and social systems. For example, a model could help analyze how policies affect different economic groups. However, having access to lots of sensitive data means there are responsibilities around using that data. Data scientists need to protect privacy, avoid bias, and use data ethically. Lots of people are nervous and upset about how much data is collected about them on a regular basis. For example, you might talk about wanting a new bed and then the next day get a bunch of ads on Google about new beds. Many people find this alarming so it is important for data scientists to learn about what data is ethically acceptable to collect, analyze, and publish. It is also important to ensure people know how their data is being collected and used. Additionally, data scientists need to make sure that data accurately represents the people that are being studied. In this course, I am hoping to better understand/learn how to use machine learning, LLMs, and other methods to conduct analysis on data and be able to draw helpful conclusions on the data.
