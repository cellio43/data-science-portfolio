## About Me: Connor Elliott - Data Science Student, UNC Charlotte
I am currently a second year student at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte and I am majoring in Data Science. I enjoy working with data/statistics to find patterns in large data sets and turn them into useful information. I have learned Java, JavaScript, and a little bit of Python. My goal in data science is to work in a field that interests me and provide valuable analysis for my company as well as the general public. We live in a data driven world but without someone to interpret, organize, and present the data, it is essentially useless information. I want to be able to look at data and turn a bunch of random numbers/statistics into meaningful information that can be used to better the world. Ideally, I would want to use my degree to work for a professional sports team to provide valuable analysis on player performance, development, etc. I have been interested in sports and played sports my whole life so I could provide lots of passion in that industry. However, I am open to getting experience in any field that needs a data scientist. Ideally, I want to have an internship for the summer of 2026. I am really interested in getting real work experience with data science so I can try to figure out the specific field that I want to work in.
